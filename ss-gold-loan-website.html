Questions people ask first

How much can I borrow against my gold?

Up to 70% of your gold’s value, depending on weight, purity and the day’s gold rate.

Which documents do I need?

An ID proof and an address proof, such as Aadhaar and PAN. No income proof is needed.

Is my gold insured?

Yes. Pledged gold is kept in insured vaults and you receive a signed receipt listing every item.

Can I close the loan early?

Yes, at any time. You pay interest only for the days the loan ran.
